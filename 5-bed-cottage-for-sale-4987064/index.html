About the property

Charming old style cottage nestled in a peaceful, private and tranquil setting in Muckross, Killarney.

This picturesque cottage is located within 2 km of Killarney National Park and is adjacent to a private pond and stream.

This property was extended in 2004 and measures 246 sqm (2,650 sq ft) with large living areas combined with natural light and old world charm.

This property is ideal for those that are looking for privacy and country life within close proximity to Killarney town.

While the property was extended in 2004, the house would benefit from some modernisation and TLC for those seeking a peaceful retreat in the popular Muckross area.

There is an attaching garage with its own separate entrance that could be used as a workspace or workshop.
